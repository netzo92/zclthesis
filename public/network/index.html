RESEARCH UPDATE / SEPTEMBER 13, 2026

52 inputs.
NonKYC addresses.

The evidence now strongly supports an internal NonKYC consolidation. Its public reserve API labels all three input addresses “Deposit” and the 2,684 ZCL destination “Withdraw.” These official labels, corroborated by valid address signatures, replace our earlier unattributed lead. The historical purpose remains an inference from those labels and the transaction.

NonKYC’s All Reserves page ↗ · Public address and signature data ↗

27 / 27 published address signatures verified

We matched the signatures to the listed ZCL address keys and independently checked them with OpenSSL. They use the Zcash message-signing format; Zclassic’s native verifier uses a different prefix and rejects them.

What changed—and what the signatures prove

Our September 12 check found empty signature data on NonKYC’s individual ZCL asset page. The separate All Reserves page and public API do publish addresses and signatures. We checked all 27 ZCL entries on September 13 at 18:28–18:32 UTC, including the four addresses in this transfer.

The signed text is “NonKYC Cryptocurrency Exchange.” It contains no balance, date, block height, wallet role or liabilities. A valid signature supports the address-key attestation; the official website supplies the organizational association. It does not prove current exclusive control, historical transaction purpose or solvency.

The 27 listed rows reported 149,575.26086405 ZCL at block 3,249,437. That is an exchange-reported, dated subset—not an independently audited reserve total. The page says addresses below $10 are not tracked. Its individual asset page reported a different, later snapshot.

52 inputs means 52 existing outputs—not 52 people

The transaction spent outputs from just three addresses. Fifty came from one address: 44 receipts of 49.99 ZCL and six of 74.99 ZCL.

Where the inputs came from

We traced all 52 parent transactions. Fifty-one involved Sapling shielded-to-transparent transfers; none was a direct coinbase reward. All fifty receipts at t1a7… came from shielded transfers between June 3 and September 5. Their on-chain fee was 0.0001 ZCL each. Being 0.01 below round amounts does not establish an exchange withdrawal fee.

The shielded side does not reveal the original senders or customers. Input count cannot establish customer count. The small 0.01028183 ZCL output was reused 64 seconds later in another consolidation sending 2,174 ZCL to the same withdrawal address.
