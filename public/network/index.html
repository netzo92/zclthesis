A transaction lead with no identified owner

Two September 5 transactions combined 50 and 52 inputs and sent round outputs of 2,416 and 2,684 ZCL to the same address. This is a consolidation pattern worth reviewing. It could come from a service, mining activity, or a holder managing UTXOs; it does not establish NonKYC ownership or common ownership of every input.
