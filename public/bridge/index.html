A token is a claim.
Backing matters.

The test design is one unit of wrapped ZCL for one unit of ZCL held by the bridge, before any configured fees. The operator controls custody and issuance. Redemption depends on that operator and its software.

This is not a trustless bridge or an audited reserve system. A service-reported balance does not prove liabilities are complete. Test assets cannot be redeemed for real ZCL.

Two networks.
Different guarantees.

Zclassic mainnet uses proof of work. Transfers of a wrapped token on Solana follow Solana’s consensus and token rules. Wrapping ZCL does not convert Zclassic to proof of stake.

This experiment uses a private regtest chain and public Solana devnet. Its balances, blocks and tokens are test data and may be reset.
