What the numbers cover

Visitors are approximate distinct browser IDs, not verified people. IDs expire after 30 days; another browser, cleared storage, or an expired ID can count as another visitor.

Visits with JavaScript disabled, Do Not Track (DNT), Global Privacy Control (GPC), or a blocking ad blocker are excluded. Automated traffic can still affect the counts.

A referral click is not a registration or a trade. The percentage above counts visits with at least one click, so repeat clicks within a visit do not increase that percentage. Events are retained for 90 days.
